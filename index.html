ELLY'S BACKSTORY

BACKSTORY

Elly grew up deep in the forest with her family, part of a forgotten tribe known as the Kamirisus. Once powerful thunder squirrel gods, feared and admired by humans, they had long since disappeared from the world and lived hidden away in the forest. When Elly was only two years old, bulldozers came and destroyed their home. She lost everything that day. Her family, and the only life she had ever known. The only ones left by her side were three little squirrels who had always watched over her: Riot, Pip and Basil. But Elly wasn't the only one who had lost everything. To the three squirrels, she was all that remained of the Kamirisus they once knew. She was their family, but also their last hope that their people wouldn't simply disappear and be forgotten. With nowhere else to go, they took Elly to the city and raised her as best they could. They protected her, taught her how to survive and, most importantly, made sure she never forgot where she came from. As Elly grew up, Riot, Pip and Basil filled her head with stories about the Kamirisus. Powerful thunder gods who once commanded the skies, feared by some humans and worshipped by others. They wanted her to be proud of what she was, but they also wanted her to become strong. Of course, hearing stories about how terrifying and powerful her ancestors used to be while the four of them were digging around for scraps and sleeping in dark alleyways wasn't exactly ideal. Still, Elly held onto every word. If Riot, Pip and Basil believed she could become something greater, then so did she. Cold, starving and constantly rejected by humans, Elly eventually made herself a promise. One day, she would become the powerful Goddess of Thunder she was always supposed to be. For now, though, she's mostly stealing nuts and anything else she can get her hands on downtown, then bringing everything back to the squat she calls home. Over time, it has become a giant mess filled with all of her favorite things. Nuts. Energy drinks. Random junk. Things that definitely belonged to someone else at some point. But She likes it.

Elly is mischievious, a bit too confident for her own good and will never miss a chance to be a flirt. After moving in her squat, growing up and learning to survive in the city, she managed to steal a cosplay costume and is now wearing it to be cool. She has in her possession her father's hammer, relic of her origins, that she wields when she is in trouble. Her thunder has a vibrant pink color, but because she is only half kamirisu, the corruption of using her thunder powers is causing her amrs to be marked. The pink electricity coursing through her veins also gave her the ability to dash really fast; so fast that she looks like she is teleporting; launching herself from the top of buildings and dashing in the night sky from building to building until she reaches the floor. When people see her steal something and get angry, she usually dashes so fast she is only leaving a trail of electriciy behind: granting her wanted posters by authority all over town. Using her thunder too much will cause her to lose control, and even though she appears to be very energetic, using extreme power surges cause her to become lethargic and depressed, addicted to nuts and energy drinks to recover and gain back her energy...That is when she is not using electrical cabinets to recharge. Though she trains to conquer the world and is very proud of her origins, DO NOT ask her who her parents were, Elly simply doesnt not want to talk about a squirrel and a human having a fling, and much less about being a hybrid to her own specie. Not fully squirrel and not entirely human, she is determined to find her place and to gain the adoration of eveyrone.
