ELLY'S BACKSTORY

BACKSTORY

Elly grew up in the forest with her family, part of a tribe most people don't even know existed: the Kamirisus. Thunder squirrel gods, once, feared by some humans and worshipped by others, before they vanished from the world altogether and went into hiding in the trees. She was only two when bulldozers tore through their home. Family, tribe, the only life she knew, gone in a day. The only ones left by her side were three squirrels who'd always watched over her: Riot, Pip, and Basil. To them, she was the last living piece of the Kamirisus. So they carried her into the city, since there was nowhere else to go, and raised her the best they could: kept her safe, taught her to survive, made sure she never forgot where she came from. As she got older, the three of them filled her head with stories about the Kamirisus, thunder gods who once commanded the skies. Hearing about how powerful her ancestors used to be while she was digging through trash and sleeping in alleyways wasn't exactly inspiring, but she held onto it anyway. Cold, hungry, and turned away by humans more times than she could count, she made herself a promise: one day she'd become the Goddess of Thunder she was always meant to be. For now, though, she mostly steals nuts and whatever else she can grab downtown, then drags it all back to the squat she calls home....Or asks her squirrels to do it for her. Over the years the squat has turned into a giant mess: nuts, energy drinks, random junk, stuff that definitely belonged to someone else at some point. But she wouldn't have it any other way.

Elly's mischievous, a little too confident for her own good, and never misses a chance to flirt. Somewhere along the way she stole a cosplay costume and started wearing it just because it looks cool. She also carries her father's hammer, a relic of where she comes from, and pulls it out when things get rough. Her thunder runs a vibrant pink, but since she's only half Kamirisu, using it corrupts her a little each time, leaving marks that creep up her arms. That same pink electricity lets her move insanely fast, fast enough to look like she's teleporting, launching herself off rooftops and cutting across the night sky building to building. When someone catches her stealing and tries to chase her down, she's gone before they can react, nothing left behind but a trail of electricity. Nobody's ever actually caught her, and so.....the cops have papered the whole city in wanted posters just hoping somebody will. She fights bad guys sometimes...without ever wanting to- But push her too far, though, and she loses control of everything. For someone who seems so full of energy, going too hard on her powers wrecks her, leaves her lethargic, cynical and depressed. She's more or less hooked on nuts and energy drinks to recover, or she'll plug herself into an electrical cabinet if she has to. She trains hard and wears her origins with pride, but there's one topic that's completely off-limits: her parents. She doesn't want to talk about a squirrel and a human hooking up, and she wants even less to talk about being a hybrid within her own species. Not fully squirrel, not fully human, she's still figuring out where she fits, and she is determined to gain the world's admiration...Even if it's just through a screen.
